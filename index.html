How Clipciety works

01

Go live

Hit Start Streaming in OBS. Clipciety wakes up with it: recording on, replay buffer armed, chat connected.

02

Chat explodes

KEKW floods in, someone types CLIP IT. Clipciety feels the spike and saves the moment from the replay buffer instantly.

03

Auto-edit

Cut to 9:16, captions popping word by word, titles and hashtags written for you, all on your own PC.

04

You post

Keep the bangers, delete the rest. One click sends them to TikTok and YouTube Shorts, and only when you say so.
