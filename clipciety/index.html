How Clipciety works

01

Go live

Hit Start Streaming in OBS. Clipciety wakes up with it: recording on, replay buffer armed, chat connected.

02

Chat explodes

KEKW floods in, someone types CLIP IT. Clipciety feels the spike and saves the moment from the replay buffer instantly.

03

Auto-edit

Cut to 9:16, captions popping word by word, titles and hashtags written for you, all on your own PC.

04

You post

Keep the bangers, delete the rest. One click sends them to YouTube Shorts, and every clip is saved ready to upload to TikTok, only when you say so.

How it works

Stream. We'll handle the clips.

Starts when you go live

Clipciety connects to OBS on your PC. When your stream starts, it makes sure OBS is recording and starts watching your chat.

Clips the hype as it happens

When chat explodes, or you press Clip it, Clipciety saves that moment from OBS's replay buffer and adds it to your review queue.

Finds the best moments

After your stream it reads your voice, your chat and the gameplay itself (kill feeds, round banners, explosions), tuned to your game type. The best moments become 1080×1920 vertical videos with word-by-word captions, plus suggested titles, captions and hashtags.

You choose what to post

Keep or delete each clip. Post the ones you keep to YouTube Shorts with one click, or download them for TikTok. Nothing is ever posted without you.
