Clipciety by Sinciety

Your best stream moments, clipped and ready to post

Clipciety is a desktop app for Windows that works alongside OBS. It catches the moments your chat goes wild while you stream, turns them into vertical clips with captions, and gets them ready for TikTok and YouTube Shorts. You decide what gets posted.

How it works

Starts when you go live

Clipciety connects to OBS on your PC. When your stream starts, it makes sure OBS is recording and starts watching your chat.

Clips the hype as it happens

When chat explodes, or you press Clip it, Clipciety saves that moment from OBS's replay buffer and adds it to your review queue.

Edits automatically

The best moments become 1080×1920 vertical videos with word-by-word captions, plus suggested titles, captions and hashtags.

You choose what to post

Keep or delete each clip. Post the ones you keep to TikTok or YouTube Shorts with one click. Nothing is ever posted without you.

Private by design

Your recordings and clips stay on your PC. Clipciety has no servers that store your videos.

Works with your platforms

Connect Twitch for live chat, and TikTok or YouTube to post. Disconnect any account from the app at any time.
