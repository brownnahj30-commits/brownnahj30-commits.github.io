Privacy Policy

Sinciety apps · Effective November 6, 2026

This policy explains what data Sinciety's apps handle and where it goes: Clipciety (including Sinciety CHAT and Voiciety), Sinciety COMMAND and Sinciety AI (PULSE). In short: your recordings, clips, chat and PC data stay on your computer; our online services keep only what they need to run your plan, connect Kick, and answer Sinciety AI; nothing is posted or streamed unless you choose to; and we don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Sinciety AI is being introduced in stages. The parts of this policy about it apply once you start using it.

1. Data that stays on your computer

The apps store the following only on your own PC (on Windows, in %APPDATA%\com.sinciety.clipciety, COMMAND's data folder, and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sinciety):

  • Your recordings' locations and the clips, thumbnails and vertical videos Clipciety creates.
  • Chat messages, gifts and follows from your streams, viewer counts sampled while you're live, and the highlights and statistics calculated from them.
  • Your settings, scenes, alert rules, automations and multistream setup.
  • PC performance data COMMAND reads (processor, graphics, memory, disk, network use and temperatures). It's used to show you graphs and alerts and isn't sent to us.
  • Sign-in tokens for platforms you connect, stream keys, your licence key and any API keys you enter. Clipciety stores these encrypted; COMMAND keeps them in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Sinciety AI's memories and conversation history, in an encrypted file.

The apps talk to each other on your PC only (Sinciety Link), using a private token, for example so COMMAND can use the platform sign-ins you already made in Clipciety.

4. Voice, captions and AI writing

  • Automatic captions and Sinciety AI's listening (wake word and speech-to-text) run on your computer. Your microphone audio is not sent to us or to Anthropic; only the words it hears after you talk to Sinciety AI are sent, as text. Speech models are downloaded once from Hugging Face.
  • Voiciety and PULSE's voice are generated on your computer.
  • Clipciety's AI titles and captions use your own Anthropic API key, if you enter one. Clipciety then sends Anthropic text only: why a moment was detected, nearby chat messages, and the clip's transcript if there is one. Your video is never sent.

5. Data from TikTok

The apps don't sign in to TikTok or post to it. Data read from your TikTok LIVE (public comments, gifts, likes and follows) is used only to show and read out your chat, is stored only on your computer, and is not sold, shared with other parties or used for advertising. Turning TikTok LIVE off stops it straight away.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete clips and broadcasts (with their chat and statistics) in Clipciety. Your original recordings are never deleted by the apps.
  • Log out of a platform in the app to delete its stored sign-in. You can also revoke access in that platform's settings. Logging out of Kick also deletes your sign-in from the Kick relay.
  • View, edit and delete Sinciety AI's memories in its settings, and clear its conversation history at any time.
  • To close a paid plan, cancel it from Manage subscription. To have your billing record and Sinciety AI usage record deleted (apart from what we must keep for tax and accounting), email us.
  • Uninstalling an app removes it. To remove all remaining data, also delete the data folders listed in section 1.

7. Security

Our services use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens and keys are stored encrypted or in Windows Credential Manager, and are never put into AI requests or logs. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your licence key and PC account safe.

8. Children

The apps are not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required by the platforms you connect, whichever is higher. Sinciety AI is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from children; if you think a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Your rights

Most of your data is on your own computer, so you can view, export and delete it directly. For data our services hold (section 2), you can ask us to show, correct or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, and you can complain to your data protection authority. We'll answer requests within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. If an app starts collecting data in a new way, we'll explain it here before it happens.
