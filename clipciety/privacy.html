Privacy Policy

Clipciety by Sinciety · Effective November 4, 2026

This policy explains what data the Clipciety desktop app ("Clipciety", "the app") handles and where it goes. Clipciety is made by Sinciety ("we", "us"). In short: your recordings and clips stay on your computer, we do not run servers that collect your data, and nothing is posted to any platform unless you choose to post it.

1. Data that stays on your computer

Clipciety stores the following only on your own PC, in the app's data folder (on Windows: %APPDATA%\com.sinciety.clipciety):

  • Your stream recordings' locations and the clips, thumbnails and vertical videos Clipciety creates from them.
  • Chat messages recorded during your streams, viewer counts sampled during your streams, and the highlights and statistics calculated from them.
  • Your settings, including your OBS connection details.
  • Sign-in tokens for platforms you connect, and any API keys you enter. These are stored encrypted.

We cannot see or access this data. It stays on your computer until you delete it (see section 6).

2. Data we collect

Sinciety does not operate servers that receive your recordings, clips, chat or analytics. Clipciety does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Paid plans and creator codes. If you buy a paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe, which processes your card and billing details under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card number. Our billing service (billing.sinciety.org) stores what's needed to run your subscription: your email address, Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your plan, its status and renewal date, and your license key. For creator codes we store the code, its plan, and a note of who it was given to. To keep your plan working, the app sends your license key to billing.sinciety.org about once a day to check its status. We use this information only to provide your plan, handle billing and support, and meet legal and tax obligations, and we don't sell it.

To check for updates, Clipciety downloads an update file and installers from GitHub, which is operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub receives standard technical information such as your IP address when this happens, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not receive information that identifies you from these checks.

3. Platforms you choose to connect

You can connect accounts so Clipciety can work with them. Each connection uses that platform's official sign-in, asks for your permission, and can be disconnected in Clipciety (Integrations → Log out) or in the platform's own account settings at any time.

4. AI features

Writing titles, captions and hashtags with AI is optional and only works if you enter your own Anthropic API key. When you use it, Clipciety sends Anthropic text only: the reasons a moment was detected, chat messages from around that moment, and a transcript of what was said in the clip if one exists. Your video is never sent. Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Automatic captions use speech recognition that runs on your computer. The speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face; your audio is not sent anywhere.

5. Data from TikTok

Data Clipciety receives from TikTok (your basic profile and the results of posting) is used only to provide the features above, is stored only on your computer, and is not sold, shared with other parties or used for advertising. Disconnecting TikTok in Clipciety deletes the stored TikTok sign-in from your computer.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete individual clips, or all skipped clips, in the app. Their files are removed from your computer. Your original stream recordings are never deleted by Clipciety.
  • Delete a broadcast in the app to remove its clips, chat and statistics.
  • Log out of a platform in Integrations to delete its stored sign-in. You can also revoke access in that platform's settings.
  • To close a paid plan, cancel it from Manage subscription. To have your billing record deleted (apart from what we must keep for tax and accounting), email us.
  • Uninstalling Clipciety removes the app. To remove all remaining data, also delete the data folder listed in section 1.

7. Children

Clipciety is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required by the platforms you connect, whichever is higher.

8. Your rights

Because your data is stored on your own computer, you can view, export and delete it directly. If you have a question or request about your data, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. If Clipciety starts collecting data in a new way, we'll explain it here before it happens.
