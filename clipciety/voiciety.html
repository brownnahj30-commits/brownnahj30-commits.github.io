How it works

On screen in two minutes

Start Voiciety

Open the Voiciety tab in Clipciety and press Start. It joins your Twitch chat instantly, with no extra login needed.

Add it to OBS

Copy your private overlay link into an OBS Browser source. The walkers and voices appear on your stream.

Chat takes over

Viewers pick a character and start talking. Every chatter gets their own walker with their name above their head.

Chat commands

!voice fizzPick your character. It's remembered for next time.
!say hello chatSpeak with your character. Your walker glows and talks.
!voicesShow the cast on screen.
!skipMods and the streamer can cut a line short.
Free and premium

Voiciety runs on your own PC, so there's no cloud bill behind every message. The voices download once and then work offline.

Built to stay safe

Links are never read aloud. You choose who can talk (everyone, subs or mods only), set cooldowns and a length limit, add blocked words, and mods can skip any line. Your overlay link has its own private key that you can reset anytime.
